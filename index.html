    Abilities

      How to Play

      CREWMATE
      Learn the rooms and find their task terminals. Press E at a station to complete five brain-training tasks. Then help the crew with support tasks. Finish all tasks or vote out the impostor to win. Ghosts can still work.

      IMPOSTOR
      Press Q to KILL a nearby crewmate, with a 30-second cooldown. E also attacks when a target is in range. H cloaks you for 5 seconds, once per round; killing is disabled while cloaked. Z closes a nearby door. C sabotages comms, X reactor, and V uses a nearby VENT. The ship schedules two 15-second outages with a five-second warning; impostor vision is unaffected.

      MEETINGS
      Meetings last up to 30 seconds. R reports a nearby body. Space calls a meeting at the Cafeteria table. Type an alibi and tap a player to vote. Tap again to cancel; tap someone else to change your vote. Room alibis are unverified claims. A tie or no votes ejects no one.

      CONTROLS
      WASD / arrow keys / right-hand touch joystick to move. On phones, buttons appear only near targets. Tap the Cafeteria emergency button directly. The Abilities button opens impostor skills. Settings contains help and sound. E to interact or attack. There is no map. Remember room locations. Esc closes a task and saves its checkpoint. P pauses solo play.

      PLAY TOGETHER
      Play finds a public waiting room. Create Room gives friends a private code and invite link. The host starts with 1–8 humans; bots fill the remaining slots. Choose your unique suit in lobby settings.

      SHIP EMERGENCIES
      Two different crewmates must activate reactor terminals within 10 seconds. Bots can help. Repair the final emergency before time runs out. Late joiners spectate until the next round.

      A host change returns the group to the lobby. Guest play is always available; signing in is optional.
